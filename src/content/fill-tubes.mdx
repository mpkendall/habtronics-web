Habtronics offers an all-in-one balloon fill kit to safely and easily fill your high-altitude balloons. These kits come with a Preece Precision CGA-580 pressure regulator to safely reduce the pressure from your helium tank down from 3000 psi to 80 psi. The hose is a ½ in. diameter Flexilla hybrid polymer hose. These hoses combine the lightness of PVC with the complete memory-free kink-free performance of a rubber hose, and are rated for -40° to 140°F. 

<img src="/images/FillTube_Hose.png" alt="Fill Tube Hose" class="w-[95%] md:w-[90%] mx-auto" />

Via brass hose barb, we attach a custom-built balloon neck adapter in 2 sizes, depending on the size of your balloon.

<img src="/images/FillTube_AdapterSizes.png" alt="Fill Tube Adapter Diagram" class="w-[95%] md:w-[90%] mx-auto" />

We also affix a label listing the approximate weight of the balloon adapter plus 4 ft. of hose to help accurately calculate your fill. These adapters are made from furniture grade polished PVC, and we manually add a smooth beveled edge to prevent any possibility of damaging the balloon.

<img src="/images/FillTube_Chamfers.png" alt="Fill Tube Adapter Closeup" class="w-[95%] md:w-[90%] mx-auto" />

These adapters and handmade by our family team, so please be aware there might be minor imperfections that do not affect the performance. To help select the right size, we offer this guide, based on the Kaymont balloons we recommend:

| Kaymont Balloon Size | Recommended Adapter Size |
|--------------|-------------------------|
| HAB-150 to HAB-1500 | 3 cm |
| HAB-1800 to HAB-4000 | 5 cm |
