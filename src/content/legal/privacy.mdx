## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

### Information you provide directly
- Contact information such as your name and email address when you contact us.
- Message content and any files or details you submit through our contact form.
- Order-related information you provide during checkout, such as shipping details.

### Information collected automatically
- Technical and usage data such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and referring URLs.
- Basic diagnostics and security logs used to keep the website reliable and secure.

### Payment information
Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers. Stripe processes payment and related transaction information in accordance with Stripe's privacy practices.

Stripe Privacy Policy: [https://stripe.com/privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy)

## 2. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:
- Process and fulfill purchases, returns, and customer support requests.
- Communicate with you about orders, product questions, and service updates.
- Operate, maintain, and improve the performance and security of our Services.
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
- Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Contact Form Processing

When you submit our contact form, your information is sent through Formspree so we can receive and respond to your message. Your submission is subject to Formspree's terms and privacy practices.

Formspree Privacy Policy: [https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy)

## 4. Cookies and Local Storage

Our Services may use browser storage technologies (including local storage) to support core functionality, such as storing cart data between pages.

You can clear local storage through your browser settings. Doing so may remove cart/session data and affect site behavior.

## 5. When We Share Information

We may share personal information only as needed in the following cases:
- **Service providers:** with trusted vendors that help us process payments, receive contact messages, host or support site operations.
- **Legal compliance:** when required to comply with law, legal process, or enforceable governmental requests.
- **Business transactions:** in connection with a merger, sale, financing, or acquisition involving some or all of our business.

We require service providers to handle information securely and only for authorized purposes.

## 6. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including legal, accounting, tax, dispute-resolution, and operational requirements.

When information is no longer required, we delete, anonymize, or securely limit access to it.

## 7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

To make a request, email us at [info@habtronics.com](mailto:info@habtronics.com). We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.
